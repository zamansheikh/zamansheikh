Khola Chithi means "open letter" in Bangla. The idea was a small social app where you write a letter to a friend, a family member or someone you love, publish it, and let others read, like, comment on and share it.

I built the prototype over a few days in July 2024 in Flutter, with BLoC on a Clean Architecture layout and a REST data layer through Dio. I did not take it further, and the repository stays up as an archive of that experiment.
