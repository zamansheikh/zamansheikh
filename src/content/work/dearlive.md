## The product

DearLive runs on the same platform as Butterfly Live and Zimo Live, but as an independent product with its own brand, backend, database and admin console. Hosts stream or open voice rooms, viewers gift with coins and diamonds, and agencies manage their hosts through the panel.

## What we built

The app is Flutter, with [Voxa Beauty](/work/voxa-beauty/) filters and RevenueCat purchases. The API is NestJS on MongoDB and Redis. Third-party Cocos casino games from a vendor run through a mirrored games service, kept separate from the core code.

## Result

DearLive is on Google Play with more than 5,000 installs, served from dearlive.pro, with its own API and games hosts. The three repositories together have passed 390 commits, and the admin panel ships the same daily-statistics dashboards as its sister apps.
