Calcu is a calculator that hides its extra features behind long-presses instead of extra screens. Hold `=` for a precise three-decimal result, or `%` to see the remainder. Long-press a result and it is saved as a coloured button you can tap to reuse, and long-press `i` to clear them all.

It uses Material 3 with dynamic colour, has dark and light themes and no ads, and keeps history on the device with Hive. I built it in 2023 with the deCoders Family community and published it on Google Play, with signed builds and a changelog on GitHub.
