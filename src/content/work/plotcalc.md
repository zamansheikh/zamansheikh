Real-estate buyers, farmers, builders and surveyors in Bangladesh routinely deal with irregular plots and mixed units, and most calculator apps get the maths wrong for anything that is not a rectangle.

PlotCalc handles rectangles, triangles, arbitrary four-sided plots and circles with survey formulas, and switches between metres and feet everywhere. Custom polygons are drawn on screen and edited by dragging their corners. Every measurement is kept in a history with its inputs, so a figure can be checked later.

It is localised in five languages, stores everything on the device with no account or network, and ships on Google Play with a public landing page. The project has passed 130 commits and is at version 2.1.
