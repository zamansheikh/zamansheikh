Gestion is a hotel booking status app I built at Join Venture AI between January and March 2025. Its centre is a custom calendar that shows booking status at a glance.

The app uses GetX for state management, Firebase for its backend services and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. I took it through release on both Google Play and the App Store while leading the Flutter team.
