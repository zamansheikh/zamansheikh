## The client

BowlersNetwork Inc. is building a home for the bowling community: a place to track scores, build a reputation, climb leaderboards and connect with other bowlers. I worked with their team remotely from May 2025 into 2026.

## What I built

I built the BowlersNetwork mobile app in Flutter and its web platform in Next.js. The app follows clean architecture with BLoC for state, GoRouter for navigation, GetIt for dependency injection and Dio for the REST layer. The web app is Next.js with TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, sharing the brand's green design system with the app.

Much of the work was complex UI and animation: feeds, profiles with levels and follower counts, and score views. I integrated the REST APIs and Firebase services, and worked with a cross-functional team to define, design and ship new features across web and mobile.
