National University of Bangladesh has more than two million students and no simple way for them to track their own results. NU Result lets a student enter each year's courses, credits and grades and watch their SGPA and cumulative CGPA update as they type, using the university's A+ to F scale.

The transcript generator turns that data into a clean, university-style academic transcript as a PDF, produced on the device. Analytics screens chart SGPA by year, credit distribution and the best and worst terms, so a student sees the trend and not just the number. A companion service scrapes the official results portal, solving its captcha automatically, so results can be fetched as structured JSON.

I led the deCoders Family team that built it. It is on Google Play with more than a thousand installs.
