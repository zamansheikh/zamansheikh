Campus Saga started as a way to make university life in Bangladesh more accountable. A student posts an issue from campus, a broken lab, a scheduling clash, a harassment complaint, and other students discuss it in the open and vote on whether it is credible. Verified university admins respond, mark solutions and are rated on how they handled it, and those ratings feed an institutional ranking.

It was my final-year project, built with the deCoders Family team I led. The app is Flutter with Clean Architecture and BLoC on Firebase, with profiles, campus analytics, a promotions feed for campus events and an admin console for moderation. It is released on GitHub with a changelog and versioned builds, and with 16 stars it is the most-starred repository on my GitHub.
