Zen Active is a fitness app I built at Join Venture AI between February and June 2025. Workouts come with videos that keep playing offline, so a workout does not depend on a connection.

Around the workouts sits a social layer of posts, groups and messaging, plus payments and cloud notifications. The app is Flutter with GetX on a REST API, and it builds through a CI/CD pipeline. I led the Flutter team on it alongside Gestion.
