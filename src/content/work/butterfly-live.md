## The product

Butterfly Live is a full live-streaming social product. Hosts go live on video or open voice rooms, viewers send gifts bought with coins, agencies and families organise hosts, and rankings and levels keep people coming back. Moments, one-to-one chat and watch-together round it out.

## What we built

The mobile app is Flutter with BLoC, real-time messaging over Socket.IO, and RevenueCat for in-app purchases. Beauty filters come from [Voxa Beauty](/work/voxa-beauty/). The backend is NestJS on MongoDB and Redis, and a Next.js admin panel handles moderation, wallets, payouts and daily statistics with CSV export.

## Result

Butterfly Live is on Google Play with more than a thousand installs. The web, API and games services run on our infrastructure at butterflysm.live, and the app, backend and admin repositories together are past 360 commits.
